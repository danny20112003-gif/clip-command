Clip Command Center

Clip Command Center is a private scheduling tool that a single creator uses to manage his own TikTok accounts. It plans short gaming videos, lets the creator review each draft, and publishes approved videos to the creator's own accounts through the official TikTok Content Posting API (Direct Post).

  • Login with TikTok (Login Kit) so the creator can connect his own accounts.
  • Before every post the app reads creator_info and shows the creator's allowed privacy options and limits.
  • Videos are labeled as AI-generated content where applicable.
  • The app does not like, follow, comment or engage with other accounts.
