Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Clip Command Center ("the app") is a private tool operated by Danny (GitHub: danny20112003-gif) for managing his own TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

When an account is connected via Login with TikTok, the app receives an access token and, depending on the granted scopes, basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), account statistics (follower and like counts) and a list of the account's own videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.

How we use it

The data is used only to publish videos the creator has approved to his own accounts, to show the creator his posting schedule and statistics, and to respect TikTok's posting limits. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage

All data is stored locally in a database on the operator's own computer. It is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties. Tokens are kept only as long as the account stays connected.

Deletion

Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its tokens. You can also revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. To request deletion of any remaining data, contact us at the address below.
